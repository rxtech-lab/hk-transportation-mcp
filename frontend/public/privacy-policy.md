# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 2026**

## Introduction

HK Transport Agent ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- We collect your device's location **only when you grant permission** to find nearby bus stops and show real-time arrival information.
- Location data is processed in real-time and is **not stored** on our servers.

### Chat Data
- Conversations with the AI assistant are stored **locally on your device** to provide chat history functionality.
- Chat data is sent to our servers for processing but is **not permanently stored**.

### Usage Data
- We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience.
- This data cannot be used to identify individual users.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide real-time bus arrival information
- To find nearby bus stops based on your location
- To process your transportation queries through the AI assistant
- To improve our services and user experience

## Data Storage

- Chat history and route tracking data are stored **locally on your device** using SQLite.
- You can delete your chat history at any time from the History tab.

## Third-Party Services

- We use public transportation APIs provided by the Hong Kong government (DATA.GOV.HK) to retrieve bus route and arrival data.
- AI processing is handled by our backend servers.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data stored on your device
- **Delete** your chat history and tracking data
- **Revoke** location permissions at any time through your device settings

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@rxlab.app.
